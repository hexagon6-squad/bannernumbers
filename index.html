How to print
  1. Fill in the bannerPick your numbers Click a box on the banner, then tap a number in the Fill the box row. Open "Numbers you need" to see the count. Tap 0-9 or type a set. Each number is one page.
  2. Click PrintThe green Print button at the bottom of this panel opens the PDF preview in a new tab. For the sign itself, use "Print banner mockup."
  3. Click print in the previewUse the print icon in the PDF preview tab. If no tab opens, allow pop-ups, or use Download PDF and open the file.
  4. Print at Actual sizeLetter paper, Scale Actual size or 100%. Not "Fit."
  5. Cut on the crop marksEach number trims to 6 x 9 in. The faint gray line shows the edge.
