Defaults match the Tree Lighting banner file: 8.5 in tall, 90% width. Trim line 0% = off. 20% is a very faint gray that just guides the cut.

In the print dialog set Scale: 100% (or "Actual size") and Margins: None. Choose "Save as PDF" to send it to someone else.
